# Uber Organizational Culture & Leadership Analysis
This project presents a critical analysis of Uber’s organizational culture, leadership challenges and employee motivation issues using organizational behaviour and HR management theories.
## Project Focus
- Leadership Analysis
- Organizational Culture
- Employee Motivation
- Workplace Ethics
- Employee Retention
- HR Management
## Frameworks Used
- Schein’s Organizational Culture Model
- Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs
- Vroom’s Expectancy Theory
- Adams’ Equity Theory
- Transformational Leadership
- Toxic Leadership Theory
## Key Insights
- Leadership behaviour strongly influenced workplace culture
- Lack of ethical leadership reduced employee trust
- Organizational culture impacted employee motivation and retention
- HR systems failed to support employee wellbeing
